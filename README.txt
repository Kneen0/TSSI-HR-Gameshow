YOUR OWN MUSIC (optional)
=========================
Put MP3 files in this folder with these exact names (lowercase). Each one
replaces the built-in tune for that moment. Any you leave out keep the
built-in music, so you can add just one or two.

  lobby.mp3      Lobby while people join, and the betting screen   (loops, quiet)
  think.mp3      While a question's countdown clock is running      (loops, quiet)
  gallop.mp3     During the horse race                              (loops, quiet)
  cards.mp3      Jingle: Play Your Cards Bright intro               (plays once, max 9 s)
  price.mp3      Jingle: The Price is Bright intro                  (plays once, max 9 s)
  derby.mp3      Jingle: The Desk Derby intro                       (plays once, max 9 s)
  fortunes.mp3   Jingle: Global Fortunes intro                      (plays once, max 9 s)
  board.mp3      Sting: leaderboard / race finish                   (plays once, max 6 s)
  victory.mp3    Grand finale fanfare                               (plays once, max 20 s)

Longer tracks are fine: jingles fade out automatically after their max time.
Keep each file under ~5 MB so the host screen loads quickly.

Where to get music you're allowed to use
- Pixabay Music (pixabay.com/music): free, commercial use OK, no credit needed.
  Search e.g. "game show", "quiz countdown", "upbeat jazz", "fanfare", "horse race".
- Don't use real TV theme tunes (e.g. the actual Price is Right or Family
  Fortunes themes). They're copyrighted and this is a company event.

After uploading to GitHub, open host.html: a message at the bottom of the
screen confirms which of your tracks it found ("Using your music for: ...").
